## How tools get listed

- **Live product only.** No waitlists, no "coming soon."
- **Public pricing or a real free tier.** If a vendor is enterprise-sales-only, we still list it, tagged `Paid (enterprise/custom pricing)`, so you know what you're getting into before you book a demo.
- **Actually used by designers**, not a general-purpose tool with one relevant use case bolted on.
- **Reviewed quarterly.** Dead links and abandoned products are removed — see [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- **No pay-to-list.** Sponsored placements are labeled `Sponsored` and never displace a better-fit free entry — see [Sponsors](#sponsors).

*Last full review: August 2026.*

## AI Tools for UI/UX Design & Prototyping

*AI UI/UX tools turn a prompt, sketch, or screenshot into editable interface designs and interactive prototypes.*

- **[Banani](https://www.banani.co)** — Turns text prompts into editable UI screens and exports clean HTML/CSS for designers and non-designers. `Freemium`
- **[Google Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com)** — Gemini-powered tool that turns prompts, sketches, or screenshots into UI screens with Figma and code export. `Free`
- **[Uizard](https://uizard.io)** — Turns sketches, screenshots, or text prompts into editable UI mockups and interactive prototypes. `Freemium`
- **[UX Pilot](https://uxpilot.ai)** — Generates multi-screen UI flows from a text prompt and exports directly to Figma. `Freemium`
- **[v0](https://v0.app)** — Vercel's AI tool that generates functional React UI components and app scaffolding from prompts. `Freemium`
- **[Visily](https://www.visily.ai)** — Converts text prompts, sketches, or app screenshots into editable wireframes and prototypes. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Logo & Brand Identity Generation

*AI logo generators turn a business name and style preferences into a set of logo options and matching brand assets.*

- **[Brandmark](https://brandmark.io)** — Generates logo concepts bundled with brand guidelines, fonts, and color palettes from a business description. `Paid`
- **[Designs.ai](https://designs.ai)** — Generates a logo plus matching brand assets like business cards and mockups from a text prompt. `Freemium`
- **[Logo.com](https://logo.com)** — Generates logo concepts from a business name and style prompt, with free downloads on the entry plan. `Freemium`
- **[Looka](https://looka.com)** — Generates logo options from a business name and style preferences, plus a matching brand kit. `Paid`
- **[Tailor Brands](https://www.tailorbrands.com)** — AI logo and brand kit generator bundled with a website builder and US LLC formation tools. `Paid`

## AI Tools for Image Generation & Illustration

*AI image generators turn a text prompt into original artwork, illustrations, or photorealistic images for creative use.*

- **[Adobe Firefly](https://firefly.adobe.com)** — Generates images, vectors, and text effects trained on licensed content, integrated across Adobe apps. `Freemium`
- **[Ideogram](https://ideogram.ai)** — Generates images with accurate embedded text, suited for logos, posters, and typographic designs. `Freemium`
- **[Krea](https://www.krea.ai)** — Real-time image and video generation canvas that bundles multiple third-party AI models in one interface. `Freemium`
- **[Leonardo.Ai](https://leonardo.ai)** — Image generation platform with fine-tuned style models and canvas editing for game and product art. `Freemium`
- **[Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com)** — Generates stylized images from text prompts via Discord or a web app, widely used for concept art. `Paid`
- **[Minglyph](https://minglyph.com/)** — Creates PNG word-art concepts from text, meaning and visual style for designers exploring poster and cover lettering. `Freemium`
- **[NightCafe](https://nightcafe.studio)** — AI art generator combining several image models with daily free credits and a creator community. `Freemium`
- **[Playground](https://playground.com)** — AI image editor and generator with canvas-based compositing and custom model training. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Presentation & Slide Design

*AI presentation tools turn a prompt or outline into a formatted slide deck, adjusting layout and design automatically.*

- **[Beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai)** — Applies design rules automatically as you type so slides stay aligned and on-brand. `Paid`
- **[Canva Magic Design](https://www.canva.com/magic-design/)** — Generates a full slide deck from a text prompt or existing document inside Canva's editor. `Freemium`
- **[Decktopus](https://www.decktopus.com)** — Generates presentation decks from a text prompt with built-in AI copywriting and design suggestions. `Freemium`
- **[Gamma](https://gamma.app)** — Generates a formatted slide deck, document, or webpage from a prompt or outline in about a minute. `Freemium`
- **[Pitch](https://pitch.com)** — Collaborative deck-building tool with AI slide generation plus built-in commenting and analytics. `Freemium`
- **[Plus AI](https://plusai.com)** — AI add-on for Google Slides and PowerPoint that generates and reformats decks inside the native app. `Paid`

## AI Tools for Mockup & Product Photography

*AI product-photography tools turn a single product photo into studio-style images, backgrounds, and marketing mockups.*

- **[Claid.ai](https://claid.ai)** — API and app for batch background removal, image upscaling, and studio-style product photo generation. `Freemium`
- **[ClipDrop](https://clipdrop.co)** — Stability AI's toolkit for background removal, image upscaling, and AI-generated product scenes. `Freemium`
- **[Freepik AI](https://www.freepik.com)** — Includes Magnific-powered upscaling and relighting alongside AI image generation and mockup templates. `Freemium`
- **[Pebblely](https://pebblely.com)** — Generates studio-style product photo backgrounds from a single uploaded product image. `Freemium`
- **[Photoroom](https://www.photoroom.com)** — Removes backgrounds and generates product photos, mockups, and marketing images from one photo. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Color Palette & Design Systems

*AI color tools generate palettes, brand color systems, and accessibility-checked contrast pairs from a prompt or preference set.*

- **[Colormind](https://colormind.io)** — Generates color schemes trained on photos, movies, and popular art, with individual color locking. `Free`
- **[Coolors](https://coolors.co)** — Generates and fine-tunes color palettes with an AI-assisted mode and export to major design tools. `Freemium`
- **[Huemint](https://huemint.com)** — Generates full brand color systems for logos, websites, and illustrations using constrained AI search. `Free`
- **[Khroma](https://www.khroma.co)** — Learns your color preferences from an initial set of picks, then generates unlimited matching palettes. `Free`
- **[Stark](https://www.getstark.co)** — Accessibility toolkit with AI alt-text and contrast checking built into Figma, Sketch, and browser workflows. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Vector Graphics & Icon Generation

*AI vector tools generate icons and scalable graphics from a prompt, or convert raster images into clean, editable vectors.*

- **[Iconscout](https://iconscout.com)** — AI icon and illustration generator layered on a large stock library, with Figma and Sketch plugins. `Freemium`
- **[IconVectors](https://iconvectors.io/)** — Desktop SVG editor that lets designers edit icons through Claude Desktop via MCP and export PNG assets. `Paid`
- **[Kittl](https://www.kittl.com)** — Vector design tool with a prompt-to-vector generator, AI vectorizer, and template editor for graphic design. `Freemium`
- **[Recraft](https://www.recraft.ai/generate/icons)** — Generates vector icons and illustrations from text prompts with brand style and color controls, exportable as SVG. `Freemium`
- **[SVGMaker](https://svgmaker.io)** — Generates and edits scalable vector graphics from a text prompt, with raster-to-SVG conversion. `Freemium`
- **[Vectorizer.AI](https://vectorizer.ai)** — Converts raster images like PNG and JPG into clean, scalable vector graphics via AI, with API access. `Freemium`

## AI Tools for Video & Motion Design

*AI video tools generate motion graphics, talking-head avatars, or clips from a text prompt, image, or script.*

- **[HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com)** — Generates AI avatar videos with voice translation and lip-sync for marketing, training, and social content. `Freemium`
- **[Luma Dream Machine](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine)** — Generates short video clips from a text prompt or image with the Ray2 model. `Freemium`
- **[Pika](https://pika.art)** — Generates and edits short video clips from text prompts or images with effect presets. `Freemium`
- **[Runway](https://runwayml.com)** — Generates and edits video from text or image prompts with character-consistent scenes for production pipelines. `Freemium`
- **[Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io)** — Generates AI avatar presenter videos from text scripts for marketing and training content. `Freemium`
